# Lost or Stolen Card Procedures

## Immediate Steps

If you lose your card or believe it has been stolen, take the following steps immediately:

1. **Report the loss** - Contact our support team to report your lost or stolen card
2. **Request a claim** - We will open a support ticket to investigate the incident
3. **Monitor your accounts** - Review your recent transactions for any unauthorized activity

## What Happens Next

Once you report a lost or stolen card:

- A support ticket is created with **HIGH** priority
- Your card will be flagged for review
- You will receive a replacement card within 5-7 business days
- Any unauthorized transactions will be investigated

## Important Notes

- We do not offer instant card blocking through this AI assistant
- For urgent concerns, please call our 24/7 hotline at 1-800-BANK-HELP
- Fraudulent charges may be reversed after investigation
- You will be notified via email once your replacement card ships

## Timeframes

| Action | Timeframe |
|--------|-----------|
| Support ticket created | Immediate |
| Card flagged for review | Within 1 hour |
| Replacement card shipped | 5-7 business days |
| Fraud investigation | 5-10 business days |
