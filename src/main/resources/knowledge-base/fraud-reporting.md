# Fraud Reporting and Suspicious Activity

## Reporting Fraud

If you suspect fraudulent activity on your account:

1. **Contact us immediately** - Report suspicious transactions as soon as possible
2. **Provide details** - Include dates, amounts, and merchant names if known
3. **We will investigate** - Our fraud team will review all flagged transactions

## Suspicious Activity Indicators

Be alert for:

- Transactions you did not make
- Unauthorized changes to your account information
- Unexpected account freezes or restrictions
- Emails or calls claiming to be from us asking for personal information

## Our Fraud Protection

- We monitor transactions for unusual patterns
- Unauthorized transactions may be reversed after investigation
- You are not liable for unauthorized transactions reported within 60 days
- We will never ask for your password or PIN via email or phone

## PIN Reset

If you suspect your PIN has been compromised:

- Visit any branch with valid identification
- Or call our support team to schedule a PIN reset
- Your card will be temporarily disabled during the reset process

## Account Freeze

In cases of suspected fraud:

- We may temporarily freeze your account to prevent further unauthorized activity
- You will be notified via email and SMS
- The freeze is lifted once the investigation is complete and your account is verified as secure
