# Transaction Processing Times and Limits

## Processing Times

### Domestic Transactions

| Transaction Type | Processing Time |
|-----------------|-----------------|
| Purchases (POS) | Immediate |
| Online purchases | Immediate to 2 hours |
| ATM withdrawals | Immediate |
| Online transfers | 1-2 business days |
| Wire transfers (domestic) | Same business day if before 3 PM |
| Direct deposits | 1-2 business days |
| Check deposits | 2-5 business days |

### International Transactions

| Transaction Type | Processing Time |
|-----------------|-----------------|
| International purchases | 2-3 business days |
| International wire transfers | 3-5 business days |
| Foreign ATM withdrawals | Immediate (subject to limits) |

## Pending Transactions

Pending transactions appear on your account but are not yet finalized:

- They show as available balance but are not cleared
- They typically clear within 1-3 business days
- Merchants may place holds that affect your available balance
- Holds expire automatically if the transaction is not completed

## Daily Limits

### Standard Account Limits

| Limit Type | Amount |
|------------|--------|
| Daily ATM withdrawal | $1,000 |
| Daily POS purchases | $5,000 |
| Daily online transfers | $10,000 |
| Daily wire transfers | $25,000 |

### Increasing Your Limits

To request higher transaction limits:

1. Log in to online banking
2. Navigate to Settings > Transaction Limits
3. Request an increase (subject to approval)
4. Or visit any branch with valid ID

## International Transactions

- Notify us before traveling internationally
- International transactions may incur foreign exchange fees
- Some countries may be restricted based on compliance policies
- Contact us for country-specific restrictions
