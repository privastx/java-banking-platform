# General FAQ and Contact Information

## Contact Us

### Phone
- General inquiries: 1-800-BANK-HELP (24/7)
- Fraud reporting: 1-800-555-FRUD (24/7)
- Business banking: 1-800-BIZ-BANK (Mon-Fri, 8 AM - 6 PM)

### Online
- Online banking: Available 24/7 at your online banking portal
- Mobile app: Available for iOS and Android
- Live chat: Available through mobile app and online banking (Mon-Fri, 8 AM - 8 PM)
- Email: support@bankingplatform.com (response within 24 hours)

## Required Documents

### Opening an Account
- Valid government-issued photo ID (driver's license, passport, or state ID)
- Social Security Number or Tax ID
- Proof of address (utility bill, lease agreement - within last 3 months)
- Initial deposit (varies by account type)

### Applying for a Loan
- All documents required for account opening
- Proof of income (pay stubs, tax returns, or bank statements)
- Employment verification
- Credit history (we will pull this internally)

## Fees

### Account Fees
- Monthly maintenance: Varies by account type (see account policy)
- Overdraft fee: $35 per occurrence
- Returned item fee: $35 per occurrence
- Wire transfer (domestic): $15
- Wire transfer (international): $40

### Card Fees
- Replacement debit card: $5
- Replacement checkbook: $10
- Expedited shipping: $15

### How to Avoid Fees
- Maintain minimum daily balance
- Set up direct deposit
- Use in-network ATMs
- Enroll in paperless statements

## Identity Verification

To protect your account, we may verify your identity when you:
- Open a new account
- Request sensitive account changes
- Report lost or stolen cards
- Access secure messages

Accepted verification methods:
- In-person at a branch with valid ID
- Security questions set up during registration
- SMS or email verification codes
- Biometric verification via mobile app
