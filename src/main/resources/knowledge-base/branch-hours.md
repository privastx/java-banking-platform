# Branch Hours and Locations

## Standard Branch Hours

| Day | Hours |
|-----|-------|
| Monday | 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM |
| Tuesday | 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM |
| Wednesday | 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM |
| Thursday | 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM |
| Friday | 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM |
| Saturday | 9:00 AM - 12:00 PM |
| Sunday | Closed |

## Weekend Hours

- Saturday branches are open from 9:00 AM to 12:00 PM
- Limited services available on Saturdays (no wire transfers, no safe deposit box access)
- Sunday: All branches are closed

## Holiday Schedule

Branches are closed on the following holidays:

- New Year's Day (January 1)
- Martin Luther King Jr. Day (Third Monday in January)
- Presidents' Day (Third Monday in February)
- Memorial Day (Last Monday in May)
- Independence Day (July 4)
- Labor Day (First Monday in September)
- Thanksgiving Day (Fourth Thursday in November)
- Black Friday (Day after Thanksgiving) - Open 9 AM - 1 PM
- Christmas Day (December 25)

During holidays, online and mobile banking remain available 24/7.
